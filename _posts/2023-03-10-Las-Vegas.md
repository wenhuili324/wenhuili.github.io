The 2023 APS March Meeting was held in Las Vegas—a truly unique city where the first thing you see upon arriving at the airport is rows of slot machines. Vegas really comes alive at night, while the streets feel much quieter during the day. I was pleasantly surprised to find many authentic Chinese restaurants, even though I didn’t see many Chinese people around. After the meeting wrapped up, my group member Fanbo and I took a two-day trip to Grand Canyon National Park and Antelope Valley. The natural beauty was absolutely breathtaking.
