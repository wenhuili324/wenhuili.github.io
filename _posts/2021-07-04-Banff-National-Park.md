I returned to Banff National Park once again—this time with Yiling and our friend Yanwen, right after my graduation. It was more than just a trip; it was a celebration of a new chapter in life. Surrounded by towering peaks, turquoise lakes, and endless skies, I was reminded once more why I fell in love with this breathtaking land. Banff never disappoints, and my love for it only grows stronger with every visit.
