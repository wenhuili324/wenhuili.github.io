The Indiana Pacers have made it to the Finals for the first time in 25 years! As a Hoosier who lived in Bloomington for three years, I’m definitely rooting for them to win the title. This is a huge deal for the state of Indiana.

Last night, I went to a sports bar to watch Game 4 against the OKC Thunder. The place was nearly full an hour before tip-off. Throughout the game, the crowd was cheering loudly every time the Pacers scored. Even though they lost, it was an awesome experience to be part of that energy and feel the strong basketball vibe.
