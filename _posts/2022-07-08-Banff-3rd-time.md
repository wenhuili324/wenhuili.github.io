This may be my last visit to Banff National Park, and I’m grateful to have shared it with Yiling and my parents-in-law. Banff has always held a special place in my heart—its breathtaking lakes, majestic mountains, and awe-inspiring glaciers. Each visit feels like stepping into a postcard, and this one was especially meaningful, filled with beauty, reflection, and cherished company.
