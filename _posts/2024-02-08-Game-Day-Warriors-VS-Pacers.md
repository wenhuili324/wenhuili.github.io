On Feb. 8th, 2024, the Golden State Warriors, led by the Stephen Curry, came to Indy to face off against the Pacers. As expected, Curry’s presence electrified the entire arena—his popularity transcends home court boundaries, and it was clear he had fans everywhere, even in Indy (Yes, it's me!). The tickets were crazy expensive because of him, so I ended up with a seat way up high. Still, it was totally worth it. The atmosphere was incredible, and Curry didn’t disappoint—dropping an impressive 46 points and putting on a show that reminded everyone why he’s one of the greatest to ever play the game.
