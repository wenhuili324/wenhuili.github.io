Before leaving Canada, we took a memorable road trip through Toronto, Montreal, and Quebec City. From the bustling streets and skyline views of Toronto, to the rich culture and vibrant food scene in Montreal, and the charming old-world atmosphere of Quebec City, it was the perfect way to wrap up our time in Canada and create lasting memories before starting a new chapter.
