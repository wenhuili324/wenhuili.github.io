I’m moving to Lawrence, KS because Yiling is beginning her professor position at the University of Kansas. Lawrence is a vibrant college town, very similar to Bloomington, where I lived for three years. I’ve noticed that many of the houses and apartments here look quite new, with a large number built within the past two decades. The town itself feels safe and quiet, and it even offers a free gym for local residents with all the essential facilities. Lawrence is also just about an hour’s drive from Kansas City, which makes it easy and convenient for shopping, dining, and entertainment.

Overall, I really like this place.
