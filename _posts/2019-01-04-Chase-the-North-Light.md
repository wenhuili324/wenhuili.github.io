Between semesters, Yiling and I traveled to Yellowknife, a small town in Canada’s Northwest Territories, to chase the Aurora. Located near the Arctic Circle, it was definitely freezing cold. It was fascinating to see the sky so low, with daylight lasting until about 10 AM and darkness setting in as early as 3 PM. Taxis there charge by the second, which was interesting to experience. Unfortunately, we didn’t get to see the Aurora due to gloomy weather, but we enjoyed dog sledding and other fun activities.
