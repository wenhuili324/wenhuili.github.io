For this year’s Christmas holiday, we chose to escape the Midwest snow and spend a week cruising through the warm, tropical waters of the Caribbean. It was the perfect winter getaway. The food—available all day and even in formal restaurants—was absolutely delicious. The service throughout the trip was consistently excellent, and to top it off, the total cost was much lower than I expected. This cruise turned out to be an unforgettable experience, and I would highly recommend it to anyone looking for a relaxing, all-inclusive vacation.
