Banff National Park is arguably the most iconic and breathtaking national park in all of Canada. After living here for two years, I finally had the chance to visit—and it absolutely lived up to the hype. Words can hardly capture the park’s majestic beauty: towering mountains, turquoise lakes, and endless forests that feel straight out of a dream. I fell in love instantly. It’s a place I’m certain I’ll return to again.
