Took a flight back to China with a layover in Doha, Qatar. During the stop, I had the chance to take a city tour while waiting for my next flight. I was lucky to arrive in the evening—our tour guide mentioned that daytime temperatures can reach up to 120°F! At night, the breeze made the heat much more bearable. The city reminded me a lot of Las Vegas, except without the casinos on every corner.

PS: This is my first time returning to China to see my family in five years! So grateful that COVID is finally behind us!
