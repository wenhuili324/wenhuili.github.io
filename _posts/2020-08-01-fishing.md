During COVID, life came to a standstill. With nowhere to go and nothing to do, working from home felt like being cut off from the world. At one point, I joked that I had almost forgotten how to walk. But amidst the stillness, something unexpected happened—I discovered fishing. Thanks to a few adventurous friends, I picked up a rod and cast my first line. The quiet thrill of waiting, the sudden tug of a bite, and the excitement of reeling in a fish brought a sense of joy and connection. It became a peaceful escape—and a surprisingly fun new skill unlocked during uncertain times.
